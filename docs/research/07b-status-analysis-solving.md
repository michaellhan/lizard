No feature on the list is entirely missing. The gaps are in **depth and method**, not in presence.

**Analysis**
- **The analysis pipeline is real and complete end to end:** paste or bulk import → per-decision solve → grade → leaks → drills → CSV/JSON export. It runs entirely in the browser.
- **It grades more spots than GTO Wizard.** 3-way and 4-way postflop decisions are graded through live solves, while GTO Wizard skips multiway postflop. Every street, not only the river, is solved at the hero's real bet sizes.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Preflop grades carry **no EV**, because the advice comes from hand-written charts.
  - There are **three inconsistent grading scales**.
  - Only **one session's decisions are kept** at a time; the session history holds summaries only.
  - The rake is taken from the page's setting, not from the hand history.
  - 8 of GTO Wizard's 15 sites are supported.

**Custom solving**
- **Heads-up postflop solving is the strong core:** range-vs-range discounted CFR with an exact best-response exploitability. The flop is solved in an abstracted game: buckets on later streets, one turn per rank class, a river without its card.
- **Weaknesses against GTO Wizard's builder:**
  - The setup is a **hand-replay model**. There is no direct pot/stack/board/range spot builder, and custom ranges are accepted only for the ranges *entering the flop*.
  - There is **no bet-tree editor**: at most 3 sizes a street (5 in Study), shared by both players, with one raise list for every street.
  - **Nodelocking is not GTO Wizard-style.** It applies aggregate tendency shifts to a frozen equilibrium followed by a best response. There is no per-combo painting and no re-solve, and it works heads-up only.
  - **Preflop solving** works over the 169 hand classes, with postflop valued by a realization model, for 2–6 players (7 on the local server). It plays equal stacks only and no ICM.
- **Multiway:**
  - 3-way is solved.
  - 4-way is solved in a reduced game.
  - 5 or more players on the flop or turn get betting-rule heuristics. Rivers with up to 6 players are solved.

**Top recommendations for the Lizard port**
1. Wrap, don't rewrite. Keep `Advisor.advise` as the single solver entry point behind Lizard's existing `web/src/solver/protocol.ts`. Port `hh.js` and the pure core of `session.js` as TypeScript modules with their Node tests.
2. Unify grading on one documented EV-loss scale: Best, Correct, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Blunder.
3. Use `pf_study` solves for preflop EVs in analysis, and label chart-graded decisions "frequency only".
4. Add a direct spot builder (pot, stack, ranges for any street) and per-player bet trees. Both need advisor and solver changes (effort M–L).
5. Label nodelock and exploit output honestly as "best response to an adjusted equilibrium". Plan per-combo locks with re-solving as new solver work (L).

## 4. Sub-features GTO Wizard has and upstream lacks (in this scope)

**Analysis**
- **Sites:** CoinPoker, Chico, PokerBros, PPPoker, PokerMaster, WePoker and current Unibet; tracker-export import.
- **Persistent analyzed-hands database across sessions,** with a sortable hands table, date/site/stake filters, action-sequence filter and synonym search, starring, and bulk tag, note and delete. Upstream keeps one session's decisions and only summaries for the rest.
- **Preflop EV loss** (upstream preflop grades are frequency only) and a per-hand **GTO score**.
- **Hero results:** won/lost (bb W/L) alongside EV loss. The upstream analyzer tracks EV lost only [V: no won/lost fields in `session.js` `COLS`].
- **GTO Reports-style population stats:** VPIP, PFR, limp, squeeze, 5bet, "Preflop Mistakes" top 10 per stat, the Flop General View, sample-size tooltips.
- **Rake and stack auto-matching from the history** (upstream takes rake from the page setting).
- **Sharing:** a picture download of a hand, and a dedicated replayer with hotkeys and per-street jumps.

**Custom solving**
- **A direct spot builder:** arbitrary pot and stack, a turn- or river-start solve with custom ranges, PREFILL from a library spot, swap IP/OOP.
- **A range editor:** painting, weight slider or mouse wheel, saved ranges, UPI/PIO **export**.
- **A bet-tree editor:**
  - per-player and per-node sizes, geometric and x-previous-bet units;
  - always-add-all-in, force/add all-in thresholds, size merging;
  - inheritance between players and streets;
  - Tree Edit after solving.
- **Dynamic sizing** to N sizes across the whole tree.
- **Nodelocking:**
  - Set Strategy per combo and Set Frequency (overwrite modes);
  - lock or unlock by category, lock previous nodes, undo and redo;
  - Compare Nodes, Global Frequency locking;
  - locks in 3-way pots.
- **Player Profiles as in-solve incentives** across the whole tree, with saved custom profiles.
- **Preflop:**
  - 7–9-player preflop solves (upstream: 6 in the browser, 7 natively), asymmetric stacks, preflop ICM;
  - per-position open sizes, limp and cold-call options, SB complete, double and Mississippi straddles;
  - mixed sizes per raise level.
- **Depth:** to 1,000bb with SPR to 500.
- **Saved solutions:** with tags, parameter import and export, and a shareable editing URL.
- **PLO4.**

**Upstream advantages to keep** (GTO Wizard lacks these or gates them):
- Graded 3-way and 4-way postflop decisions in the analyzer.
- Every street solved at the hero's real sizes (`_swap_nearest`).
- Frequency comparisons against the mix for your actual hands.
- Explicit exploitability on every live solve.
- A fully local, offline-capable flow: browser Pyodide/WASM or the local server, with no upload.
- 5–6-player river solves.
- ICM and bounty prize-equity postflop solves.
